# Paper result tables

These CSVs reproduce the two tables displayed in the three-page poster manuscript:

- table_1_governance.csv sums the original primary governance rows in ../tables/governance.csv by method. Both distributions and S0-S3 are included. Each method has its own presented-update denominators.
- table_2_runtime.csv selects the P1-versus-B3 stable-S0 rows in ../tables/overhead.csv. Ratios divide mean round times; paired differences and confidence limits are converted from milliseconds to seconds. Full precision is retained, while the paper rounds its displayed values.

They are exports of existing aggregates, not new experiments or additional independent observations.
